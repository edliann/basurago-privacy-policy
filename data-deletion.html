User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 16, 2026

BasuraGo users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by following the instructions below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to basurago.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line BasuraGo Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your BasuraGo account.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What happens next?

After verifying your request, BasuraGo will process it within the period required by applicable law.

We will delete personal information that is eligible for deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution, or legal claims.

Third-party accounts

Deleting your BasuraGo account does not automatically delete information held independently by Facebook, Google, or other third-party services. You may need to contact those providers separately.
