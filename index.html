Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BasuraGo (“BasuraGo,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) handles personal information when you use the BasuraGo customer application and related services. BasuraGo is a waste collection and pickup scheduling platform. We aim to handle personal information transparently and in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 where applicable.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use BasuraGo and which features are enabled, information may include:

  • Account information: name, email address, profile information you provide, and identifiers used to authenticate your account.
  • Social sign-in information: information made available when you choose Facebook or Google sign-in, such as your name, email address, profile image, or provider account identifier, depending on the provider and permissions granted.
  • Pickup information: pickup address and location details you provide, waste type, bag size, quantity, instructions, requested collection date and time, and pickup status or history.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting us about the service, your account, or a privacy request.
  • Technical information: technical, diagnostic, and security information processed by the app or its service providers, to the extent these data are collected in the deployed service.

2. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to create and manage accounts; authenticate users; schedule and coordinate requested pickups; provide pickup confirmations and status updates; respond to support requests; maintain service security; troubleshoot and improve the service; and comply with legal obligations. We seek to use information for purposes relevant to providing and maintaining BasuraGo.

3. Facebook and Google Sign-In

If you choose Facebook or Google sign-in, the selected provider processes authentication under its own privacy policy. BasuraGo receives information made available through that sign-in flow and the permissions you grant, and uses it to authenticate you and associate the login with your BasuraGo account.

  • Facebook Privacy Policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Deleting your BasuraGo account does not automatically delete information held independently by Facebook, Google, or another provider.

4. How Information May Be Shared

Information may be made available to authorized personnel who need it to coordinate and complete a pickup you request, and to technology providers that support authentication, hosting, databases, storage, security, or app operation. We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights. Access should be limited to what is needed for the relevant purpose.

BasuraGo does not sell personal information in exchange for money.

5. Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No electronic system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information may be processed by service providers in locations determined by their infrastructure, subject to applicable legal requirements and relevant service arrangements.

6. Retention

We intend to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, pickup services, security, dispute resolution, legitimate business needs, and applicable legal obligations. Retention may vary by information type and purpose. Information that is no longer needed should be deleted or anonymized where appropriate and technically feasible, subject to lawful retention requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to or correction of your personal information, request deletion or erasure, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and raise a privacy concern with us or the appropriate authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your BasuraGo account and associated personal information, email basurago.support@gmail.com with the subject BasuraGo Account Deletion Request. Include the email address associated with your account. We may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership.

We will process verified requests within the period required by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution, or legal claims. Any retained information will remain subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

BasuraGo is not intended to be used by children who are not legally able to consent to the processing of their personal information without the required parent or guardian involvement. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, data practices, or legal obligations change. We will update the date above when the policy changes and provide additional notice when required by law. Please review this page periodically.
